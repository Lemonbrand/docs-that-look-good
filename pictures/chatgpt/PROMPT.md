# Your own pictures in ChatGPT (about 45 seconds each)

Attach the two style images (style-ref-1.png and style-ref-2.png, or two of your own), paste the prompt, change only the subject line.
Keep the style part the same every time: that is what makes a set of pictures look like a set.

```text
STYLE (keep this part the same every time; attach the two reference images)
Make one illustration in the exact style of the two attached images: a physical paper collage.
Cream paper cut-out with a slightly torn edge, imperfect soft-black ink outlines, colored paper
with visible fibers and halftone dots, a strip of kraft tape, a small soft shadow right under it.
One object only, centered with lots of empty space around it. Transparent background. No text, no letters.

SUBJECT (change only this line)
A signed contract with a fountain pen resting on it.
```

Subjects we tested live:
1. A signed contract with a fountain pen resting on it.
2. A weekly report sheet with a small bar chart, coming out of a printer.
3. A messy stack of papers turning into one neat folder, with a dotted arrow between them.

When one comes out right, save it in your Library folder and reuse it. Only approved pictures go in.
