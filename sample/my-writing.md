# How I write (three real examples)

Email to a new client:
"Welcome aboard. You send us receipts, we send you clean books by the 10th. If something looks off, text me, I answer the same day."

Note to the team:
"Short version: we close the month by the 8th now. Two days earlier than before. Flag anything missing by the 5th and we're fine."

Reminder to clients:
"Tax season is close. Send your T4s and slips as they arrive, not all at once in April. Snap a photo, that's enough."
