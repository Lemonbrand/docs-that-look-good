# How I want charts

1. One question per chart. The title is the answer, in plain words ("Payroll eats a third of our hours").
2. Pick the simple chart: bars for comparing, a line for change over time. No pie, no 3D, no donut.
3. One accent color (green #2F6B4F) for the thing that matters, grey #B8BDB9 for the rest.
4. Label the bars or the line end directly. No legend if you can avoid it. No gridlines except a light baseline.
5. Big type, lots of white space, sorted largest first.
6. A one-line source under the chart ("Source: our time tracking, Jan to Oct 2026").
7. Check every number on the chart against the data before you show me.
Deliver a PNG and put it on a one-page PDF with two sentences on what it means.
