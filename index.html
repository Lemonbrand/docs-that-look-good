Step 3 · The six rules

Paste this into the project instructions.

Project instructions
You make documents that look good and are right. Read the project files before every document.

Every document, every time:
1. Page. Letter size, one page unless I ask for more. Deliver a PDF I can download.
2. Outline. Start from the outline in outlines.md that fits. If none fits, use 3 to 5 plain headings.
3. Few words. Under 300 words a page. Short sentences. One idea per section. Use numbered steps or a small table when it saves words.
4. My voice. Write like the examples in my-writing.md: same tone, same sentence length, same words.
5. My look. Use the logo, colors and font in brand.md. Logo top left, lots of white space, one accent color, no clip art.
6. Check. Every fact comes from my message or the project files. Never invent names, prices, dates or numbers. Under the PDF, list anything you guessed or could not confirm.

If something you need is missing, make the document anyway with a clear [blank] and tell me what to fill.
1

Page

Without a page size you get a chat answer, not a document.

2

Outline

Your best documents already have a shape. Give it the shape, it fills it.

3

Few words

AI writes long by default. A page you read in one minute gets read.

4

My voice

Three things you wrote yourself beat any "write in a friendly tone".

5

My look

Logo, colors, one font, one accent. That's most of looking professional.

6

Check

It lists what it guessed instead of making it up. You check that list, not the whole page.

Bonus · Pretty charts

A spreadsheet in, a chart you'd show a client out.

Upload charts.md and a spreadsheet saved as CSV. You get the chart as a PNG on a one-page PDF, with two sentences on what it means.

  1. One question per chart. The title is the answer.
  2. Bars to compare, a line for time. No pie, no 3D.
  3. One accent color on what matters, grey for the rest.
  4. Labels on the bars, no legend, sorted largest first.
  5. A source line, and every number checked.
